TONIGHT'S GREAT SHIFT

AI is becoming infrastructure.

Not another clever application. Not another model launch. A deeper transition is underway — intelligence itself is becoming a basic layer of modern industry.

The interesting story is no longer just who can build the smartest model. It is what happens when capable intelligence becomes cheap enough, dependable enough, and ordinary enough to disappear into everything.

That is where new markets form: around distribution, trust, specialized workflows, proprietary data, chips, networks, power, and products nobody could afford to build a year ago.

05 · WORLD DESK

AI, chips, energy, and national strategy are becoming one story.

When computing power becomes strategically important, the conversation stops being only about software companies. Supply chains, energy, manufacturing, regulation, and geopolitics all enter the frame.

The founder takeaway is not fear. It is dependency awareness: know which parts of your product rely on infrastructure, policy, or platforms you do not control.
