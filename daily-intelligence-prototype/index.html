TODAY'S SHIFT 01

AI is moving from product to infrastructure.

What started as clever tools is becoming a core layer of the global economy.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

Think about electricity. At first it was a new invention. Then it disappeared into everything. AI may be starting the same transition: less of a single product, more of a capability built into thousands of products.

IN PLAIN ENGLISH

The important question is shifting from “Who has the smartest model?” to “What can now be built because good AI is becoming widely available?”

That pushes value toward the layers around AI: workflow tools, distribution, specialized data, trust, infrastructure and products that make the technology useful in everyday work.
