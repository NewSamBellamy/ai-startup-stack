AI is no longer just an app you use — it’s becoming the underlying infrastructure powering everything from chip design to healthcare to logistics. The next wave of value will go to those who build on top of it, not just those who wrap it in a UI.

The shift is similar to cloud computing: once the underlying capability becomes broadly available, advantage moves toward distribution, workflow fit, proprietary data, trust, and execution.
